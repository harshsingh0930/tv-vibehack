# Bundled offline photographs

These nine 1920-pixel Wikimedia Commons JPEG thumbnails are included for offline/slow-network
fallback. They retain their individual Creative Commons licences and are not MIT-licensed.

The accompanying `../heritage-wallpapers.json` contains the complete author names, original
file-page source URLs, licence names and licence URLs for each numbered JPEG. Filenames match
that catalogue's `id`. The JPEG bytes are unmodified downloads; the TV applies a centre crop
at display time and does not recolour the photographs. The source pages also provide originals.
All credits are accessible through Screensaver settings → Photo credits.

The CC BY-SA photographs remain available under their listed CC BY-SA versions, and the
CC BY photograph remains available under CC BY 4.0. Follow each catalogue entry's licence URL
for the full terms, including attribution and share-alike requirements where applicable.
