# Wallpaper provenance

`wallpaper.jpg` is an original landscape generated with OpenAI's built-in
imagegen tool for this public launcher in October 2026. It contains no app
artwork, interface, text or logos. The supplied TV image was a visual reference;
its photographed television and UI were not copied into the wallpaper.

The generated image was converted to JPEG and normalised to 1920×1080 for the
launcher. This contributed asset is distributed under the repository's
[MIT licence](../../LICENSE). No third-party photograph or stock-photo licence
is involved.
